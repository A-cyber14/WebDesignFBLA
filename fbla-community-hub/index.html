    Information you can trust.

    When someone needs food, shelter, or care, accuracy matters. Here's how we keep listings honest.

    • Checked at the source

      Every listing was verified against the organization's own website or an official government page, and links back to it.

    • Dated, never guessed

      Each listing shows the date it was verified. If a source doesn't state hours or costs, we say “Not listed.” We never fill in guesses.

    • Emergencies come first

      24/7 crisis lines are clearly marked, and emergency numbers are at the top of every page.
